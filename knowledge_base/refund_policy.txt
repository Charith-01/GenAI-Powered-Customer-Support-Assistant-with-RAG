Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase.

Refunds are normally processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned item has been received and approved.

Customers should provide their order number when requesting a refund.

Damaged or incorrect products may qualify for a full refund after verification by the support team.

Refund status inquiries should be escalated when the normal processing period has been exceeded.
